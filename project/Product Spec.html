01

What this product is

Record your voice, attach one song that holds the feeling, name it, customize the shell, send it.

This is not a mixtape app. It is a personal audio message app using cassette aesthetics. "Mixtape" is a reference point for the look, not the function.

The recipient receives a beautiful cassette that plays your voice and your chosen song — voice first, song second. That complete message is the whole product.

Everything below removes ambiguity from the earlier multi-song, Seal-driven, app-gated concept and replaces it with a single, honest, end-to-end experience designed as a complete product from day one.

02

Key decisions

Audio · no silent tapes

Spotify preview, day one

Every attached song uses Spotify's free preview_url — a 30-second clip with no OAuth and no paywall on either end. The creator searches, attaches; the recipient hears it on Side B. No streaming account required from anyone.

Sides

Side A = voice · Side B = song

Voice plays first; the recipient flips to Side B for the song. No voice note? The song moves to Side A, Side B stays blank, and the flip affordance disappears entirely — no empty side to confuse anyone. Voice is capped at a hard 0:90.

Identity

No Seal — your voice is you

The Seal system is removed. The voice note already carries identity. For visual attribution the sender's name sits in the cassette header: small uppercase "FROM" beside the name in the model-number font. Decorative, unambiguous, zero extra setup.

The label

A title, not a letter

The label holds a short 4–5 word title ("thinking of you lately"), rendered in a handwritten font on a strip of translucent paper — light cream, black text, deliberately high-contrast and accessible. There is no separate notes or message field.

Home

One action

Empty Home is a single centered (+) with one line of supporting text. No library grid, no identity prompt, no competing CTAs. The first thing a new user can do is make a tape. Library appears only after the first tape is saved.

Library

Sent + received keepsakes

One unified collection of tapes you've made and tapes you've been sent — a keepsake shelf, not a drafts folder. Saving a received tape is the natural prompt to download the app and create an account.

Account & sync

Magic link, deferred

Passwordless email login, optional, triggered only after 2–3 tapes exist. Dismiss it and the library stays local with a low-key "sync across devices" banner. Shared tapes are always server-side — the link works regardless of device or login.

Cassette literacy

Retro look, universal controls

Every control is a standard icon (▶ play, ↩ rewind) with a text label beneath. Spec text (NR ON/OFF, TYPE I, Hi-Fi Stereo) is purely decorative — never interactive, never explained. Flip is discoverable, not required: a pulsing edge glow hints it; tapping the cassette flips it.

Recipient

Web-first, no wall ★

A shared link always opens a full web player — cassette, controls, side indicator, sender name — playable immediately in any mobile browser. A dismissible Smart App Banner may appear but never blocks playback. This is the most important screen in the product.

03

Two flows

Create & send

1
Home — tap the single (+)
2
Name the tape (4–5 words) & say who it's from
3
Side A — record your voice (≤ 0:90)
4
Side B — search & attach one song (30s preview)
5
Customize the shell — color & material
6
Share the web link · Save to Library

Receive

1
Open the link — full web player, no download
2
Press play — hear the sender's voice (Side A)
3
Tap the cassette to flip to Side B
4
Hear the song preview
5
Save to your Library → prompt to get the app
